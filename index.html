    Philosophy

    Six principles from the brain.

    Today's models are powerful but narrow: they recognise without understanding, answer without checking, and break when the world changes. The brain shows a different design is possible, one that predicts before acting, grounds belief in evidence, adapts without supervision, and uses specialised senses that feed shared reasoning. We don't copy biology. We treat these principles as engineering targets, and each research direction closes one gap between how models work today and how brains work.
